# Phase 1 Employee Usage Guide

## How to Clock In

1. Open the TimeTracker app on your phone or browser.
2. You will see the **Punch Clock** section at the top of your dashboard.
3. Tap the large **CLOCK IN** button.
4. The button changes to **CLOCK OUT** and your status shows **CLOCKED IN**.
5. The current Pacific Time (PT) is displayed live.

## How to Clock Out

1. When your shift is over, tap the **CLOCK OUT** button.
2. Your status changes to **CLOCKED OUT**.
3. Your total hours for the day are calculated automatically.

## How to Take a Lunch Break

1. While clocked in, tap **START LUNCH** to begin your break.
2. Your status changes to **ON LUNCH BREAK**.
3. When you return, tap **END LUNCH** to resume your shift.
4. The primary button returns to **CLOCK OUT**.

## How to View Today's Summary

- Below the Punch Clock, the **TodayEntry** section shows your current day's detail including clock-in time, lunch times, and clock-out time.
- The **This Week (PT)** card shows your total hours and days worked for the current week.

## How to View History

- Tap **View Full History** to see all your past time entries.
- Use the back button to return to the punch clock.

## What to Do If You Forget to Clock Out

- If you forget to clock out, your shift will remain open.
- You **cannot** clock in again the next day until the previous shift is closed.
- Contact your admin/manager — they can correct the entry using the Admin Correction feature.
- The admin will need to provide a reason for the correction, which is recorded in the audit trail.

## Phone Use Notes

- The punch clock is designed for **1-2 tap** operation on mobile.
- The main button is large (full-width, 64px height) for easy tapping.
- All times are stored in **America/Los_Angeles** (Pacific Time) regardless of your device timezone.
- The app works offline — entries are saved when your connection is restored.
- Refresh the page if the live clock seems stale (auto-refreshes every 60 seconds).

## Important Rules

- **One open shift per day**: You can only have one active clock-in at a time.
- **Cannot double clock-in**: If you're already clocked in, the button shows CLOCK OUT.
- **Cannot clock out without clock-in**: The button only shows CLOCK OUT when you have an active shift.
- **Lunch requires active shift**: You can only start/end lunch while clocked in.
